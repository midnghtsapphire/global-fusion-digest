# Social drafting and publication

Create exactly six platform-specific drafts from the same completed analysis:

- **LinkedIn:** professional operational hook, short findings, practical control priorities, citations, and restrained tags.
- **Facebook:** accessible explanation, action checklist, source links, and a non-alarmist question.
- **Instagram:** carousel slide beats or caption plus a source-link handoff; do not claim an image/carousel exists unless created.
- **TikTok:** concise spoken script, on-screen text beats, caption, and source links; distinguish script from rendered video.
- **Lemon8:** title, skimmable educational cards/sections, prevention checklist, and sources.
- **Reddit:** factual title, transparent source-based body, scope/uncertainties, and community-appropriate discussion prompt; no fabricated personal incident.

Retain the severity labels 🔴 Critical, 🟧 High, 🔵 Anomaly, 🟡 Medium, ⚪ Low when useful. Never turn a sample-wide count into a claim about all threats, imply confirmed compromise at the user's organization, or reuse an old alert as breaking news. Link the full cited digest when a platform cannot carry every reference.

Before posting, confirm the user authorized the selected account and intended content. Use a real authenticated platform connector/session; login screens and public feeds are not authenticated publishing contexts. Respect subreddit/group/platform rules. Do not upload or publish in this packaging workflow; the engine and Action have no posting step.

For each platform, preserve authorization scope, checked-at UTC time, observed session state, outcome, post URL/platform ID, and published-at time when supplied. For engagement, record actual impressions/views, reactions, comments, shares/reposts/saves, click-throughs, or retention only when accessible, with observation time and metric definition. `Unavailable` is not `0`. Comparing formats requires comparable platform/time windows; do not fabricate audience growth, baselines, or optimization gains.
